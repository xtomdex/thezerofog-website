Your Caffeine Blueprint

Stop guessing when to stop - know your exact cutoff so caffeine works for your focus, not against it

The Science, in 30 Seconds

Caffeine has a half-life of 5-6 hours. Half the caffeine from your 2 PM coffee is still in your system at 8 PM. A quarter is still there at 2 AM.

You might fall asleep - but caffeine reduces deep sleep by up to 20%, even when you don't feel it.

After 2 weeks of daily use, caffeine stops giving energy - it only prevents withdrawal.

Practical Tips
  • Time it, don't quit it. Drink coffee in the morning - it works WITH your cortisol peak. After cutoff - decaf or herbal tea.
  • Hidden sources add up. Dark chocolate, green tea, pre-workout. Check the table above.
  • Know your type. ~15% are slow metabolizers (coffee at 3 PM = can't sleep). ~10% fast (coffee before bed, no problem). Rest are average. This is genetic. If 8 hours isn't enough - try a 10-12h cutoff.
  • Full reset. Reduce by 1 cup every 3 days for 2 weeks. After that, one cup hits like it used to.
