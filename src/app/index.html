Your caffeine cutoff

Enter your target bedtime to see your cutoff.

Caffeine has a half-life of 5-6 hours - half of your 2 PM coffee is still in you at 8 PM. Even when you fall asleep, it can cut your deep sleep by up to 20%. Stopping early enough keeps it out of your night.

What counts as caffeine

Everyone absorbs it differently

About 15% of people are slow metabolizers - coffee at 3 PM and they're wired at midnight. About 10% are fast - espresso after dinner, no problem. The rest are in between. It's largely genetic, and you can get it tested if you want to know your type. If an 8-hour cutoff isn't enough for you, push it to 10-12 hours.
