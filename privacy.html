Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 29, 2026 · Last Updated: March 29, 2026

The ZeroFog ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website thezerofog.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. We keep this policy straightforward because we believe you deserve clarity, not legalese.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what you voluntarily provide:

  • Email address — submitted through our opt-in form when you sign up for free training.

We do not collect your name, phone number, payment information, physical address, or any other personal data through our opt-in form. We do not require you to create an account.

Automatically collected information: When you visit our website, our servers or analytics tools may collect standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, and pages visited. This data is collected in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address to:

  • Deliver the free training you signed up for
  • Send marketing emails about ZeroFog educational content, free resources, and product launches
  • Notify you about updates relevant to the program you opted into

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your email address with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Period.

3. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. These include:

  • Using HTTPS encryption across our website
  • Storing email data with reputable, industry-standard email service providers
  • Limiting access to personal data to authorized personnel only

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following categories of third-party services that may process your data:

  • Email marketing platform (such as MailerLite or ConvertKit) — to store your email address and deliver emails. These providers have their own privacy policies and maintain industry-standard security practices.
  • Website analytics (such as Google Analytics) — we may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site. These tools collect anonymized, aggregate data. If Google Analytics is enabled, it may use cookies (see Section 6).
  • Hosting provider — our website is hosted on infrastructure that may log standard server access data (IP addresses, timestamps).

We do not use third-party advertising networks or retargeting pixels. We do not integrate with social media tracking tools.

5. Your Rights

For all users:

  • Unsubscribe — Every email we send contains an unsubscribe link. Click it and you will be removed from our mailing list. You can also email us directly at hello@thezerofog.com to request removal.
  • Access & deletion — You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it entirely. Contact us at hello@thezerofog.com and we will respond within 30 days.

California residents (CCPA): Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information — we do not sell your personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, email hello@thezerofog.com with the subject line "CCPA Request." We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days as required by law.

European visitors (GDPR): If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to access, rectification, erasure, data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Our legal basis for processing your email is your explicit consent given through the opt-in form. To exercise your rights, contact hello@thezerofog.com.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our current use of browser storage is minimal:

  • localStorage — We store your theme preference (dark/light mode) locally in your browser. This is not a cookie and is not transmitted to our servers.

We may add analytics cookies in the future (such as Google Analytics cookies). If we do, we will update this policy. Analytics cookies help us understand aggregate visitor behavior (pages visited, time on site) and do not identify you personally.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use our website.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we learn that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hello@thezerofog.com.

8. CAN-SPAM Compliance

We comply with the CAN-SPAM Act. This means:

  • We will not use false or misleading subjects or email addresses
  • We will identify marketing messages as advertisements where required
  • We will include our physical mailing address or a valid registered agent address in our emails
  • We will honor opt-out and unsubscribe requests within 10 business days
  • We will not share or sell your email address to third parties for their own email marketing

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. If we make material changes to how we handle your data, we will notify you by email (if we have your email address) or by placing a notice on our website.

Your continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, contact us:

  • Email: hello@thezerofog.com
  • Website: thezerofog.com
